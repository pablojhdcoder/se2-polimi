1.1 Purpose

This Requirements Analysis and Specification Document (RASD) provides a 
comprehensive overview of the Best Bike Paths (BBP) system. Its primary purpose 
is to serve as both a guide for developers responsible for implementing the 
system specifications and as a contractual reference point for clients and 
contractors. Additionally, it offers a clear, precise, and unambiguous 
explanation of the platform's features and limitations, empowering stakeholders 
to confirm that the system meets their needs and requirements.

The BBP system's overarching goal is to transform how cyclists discover, record, 
and share information about bike paths. To this end, it focuses on:

1. Enabling registered users to record and track their cycling trips with 
   detailed statistics and performance metrics.

2. Providing a comprehensive inventory of bike paths that can be browsed and 
   searched by all users.

3. Allowing users to contribute information about bike paths through both 
   manual and automated data collection methods.

4. Enriching trip data with meteorological information from external services 
   when available.

5. Facilitating path discovery by visualizing optimal routes between origin 
   and destination points based on path quality and effectiveness.

6. Merging and consolidating information from multiple users to provide 
   accurate and up-to-date path status information.

By offering a seamless and impactful experience, the BBP system aims to serve 
as a trusted solution that addresses the requirements of all stakeholders—
cyclists, bike associations, and urban planners—thereby ensuring a more 
efficient, safe, and enjoyable cycling ecosystem.
